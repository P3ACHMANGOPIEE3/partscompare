How this was calculated

  • Each line uses one eligible offer that covers its full quantity.
  • The suggestion is the lowest eligible recorded offer, not the lowest market price.
  • Tie rule: Lowest unit price, then highest recorded quantity, then most recent checked date.
  • The subtotal covers parts only. Shipping, travel, and other charges are excluded.
